Location

You step out of the door onto Chapora lane

Not near it — on it. Chapora Main Street is the one commercial lane running through the village, and the nearest bar sits about twenty metres from the door. It is quiet and shuttered through the day, then fills from sunset with small bars, feni and urrak counters, juice stalls and late kitchens, and stays busy well past midnight.

Short ride, by the jetty

Chapora Fort

About 700 m away on the headland above the village — a ten-minute walk to the foot of the hill, then a steep ten to fifteen minutes up to the ramparts. The standard sunset spot, looking north to Morjim and south over Vagator.

A little further

Sakana for Japanese, about 750 m. Bean Me Up, the long-running vegan kitchen in Deulvaddo, about 1.1 km. Hilltop, the open-air Vagator institution, about 1.2 km.

The address

Anjuna – Chapora, Vagator, North Goa 403509. Coordinates 15.6037° N, 73.7418° E. Manohar International Airport is around an hour by road.

Goa’s season runs roughly October to March, and some places on the lane cut their hours or close through the monsoon. Being on the main street also means it is audible late at night in season — worth knowing before you book.
