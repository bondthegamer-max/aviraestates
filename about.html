About us

One company, both sides of the key

Avira works on Goa property twice over. One division sells it. The other runs it. Most firms here do one or the other, and the seam between them is where owners lose money and guests lose patience.

Our real estate division offers land with clear title, villas and apartments. Some homes are finished and ready to move into; others are still under construction, for buyers who would rather come in early and choose from the full plan. Buy land and we will help you build on it.

Our hospitality division runs short and long-stay rentals across North Goa, including homes we had nothing to do with selling. If you already own here and the place sits empty between visits, we will come and look at it.

The two are deliberately connected. Buy a villa or an apartment through us, or buy land and have us help you build on it, and the finished home can go straight to the people in the next office — furnished, listed and earning — without appointing another company, signing another contract, or refereeing between them.

What we are for

Mission and vision

Mission

Most people buying in Goa want two things at once: somewhere they actually want to be, and something that is not sitting idle for eleven months of the year. Getting both usually takes two or three companies — one to sell you the property, one to build on it, one to run it — and the gaps between them are where the money and the goodwill go.

We close those gaps by doing all of it ourselves. Buy a finished home and we can furnish it and let it. Buy one under construction and we set it up for rental once it is handed over. Buy land and we will help you build on it, then run what you build. We sell what we would be willing to operate, and we operate what we sell. It keeps us honest at the point of sale, because the rental numbers we quote are numbers we then have to go and earn.

Vision

North Goa is being built a great deal faster than it is being run. Handing over keys is easy. Still answering the phone three seasons later — when the pump has failed and a guest is standing in the driveway — is the part most operators quietly stop doing.

We want Avira to be the name owners and guests reach for because the operating holds up, not because the brochure was good. Get that right and the buildings look after themselves.
